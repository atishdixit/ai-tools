# Resources

Courses, books, documentation and communities, by branch. Free options are marked **(free)**; many courses are free to follow and
charge only for certificates. Books are listed by author and title without links; find them at your library or bookshop.

[← Back to the roadmap](../README.md)

> **Links and availability change.** Links were checked when this file was last updated (October 2026). If one breaks, search for the
> title, and please open an issue.

## How to choose

- Pick **one main course per branch** and finish it; add others only to fill gaps.
- Prefer resources with **exercises and projects** over passive videos.
- Official documentation is the most reliable source for tools; check its date.
