# Products and pricing

All prices are in Indian rupees (INR), per month, and exclude GST. GST is charged extra at 18 percent.

## RouteWise

RouteWise plans delivery routes for fleets of vehicles. It cuts distance and fuel use by choosing the best order of stops.

| Plan | Price per month | Vehicles included |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Starter | Rs 4,999 | up to 5 vehicles |
| Growth | Rs 14,999 | up to 25 vehicles |
| Enterprise | Custom price, ask Sales | unlimited vehicles |

Every RouteWise plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

## StockSense

StockSense forecasts demand and tells warehouse teams what to reorder and when.

| Plan | Price per month | Warehouses included |
|------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Basic | Rs 7,999 | up to 2 warehouses |
| Pro | Rs 19,999 | up to 10 warehouses |

The Pro plan also includes access to the demand forecasting API. StockSense includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card needed.

## Dockly

Dockly schedules truck arrivals at warehouse docks so that trucks do not queue.

Dockly costs Rs 9,999 per month for each site. The price includes SMS alerts to drivers, up to 2,000 SMS per month.
Extra SMS messages cost Rs 0.30 each. Dockly has no free trial, but Sales offers a free 30-minute live demo.

## Add-ons

- Premium support: Rs 3,000 per month. It gives round-the-clock priority 1 response (see the support policy).
- Onboarding workshop: Rs 25,000, charged once.

## Billing

- Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount compared with paying monthly.
- Invoices are payable within 15 days.
- Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, unused months are not refunded.
- Customers can upgrade a plan at any time and the change applies immediately. A downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle.
