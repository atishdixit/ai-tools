# HR policy: leave, work hours and benefits

## Leave

- Annual leave: 22 paid days per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Up to 10 unused days carry over to the next year.
- Sick leave: 10 paid days per year. A medical certificate is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 26 weeks for the parent giving birth and 4 weeks for the other parent.
- Public holidays: 12 per year. The list is published every December.

## Working hours and place of work

- Standard hours are 9:30 to 18:30 with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours, when everyone should be available, are 11:00 to 16:00.
- Zenith Cloudworks follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office 3 days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.
- Employees may work from home on up to 20 additional days per year with their manager's approval.

## Notice period

The notice period is 60 days for employees and 90 days for managers.

## Benefits

- Learning budget: Rs 30,000 per employee per year for courses, books and conferences.
- Internet allowance: Rs 1,000 per month for employees who work from home.
- Health insurance covers the employee, spouse and up to two children.
