# Customer support policy

## How to reach support

- Email: support@zenithcloudworks.example. The mailbox is monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Phone: +91 20 5550 0142, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time (IST).
- Live chat inside the product: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 IST.

## Response times

| Priority | Meaning | First response target |
|----------|---------|-----------------------|
| P1 | The system is down or unusable | 30 minutes, around the clock, for Enterprise customers and customers with Premium support |
| P2 | A major feature is broken, with a workaround | 4 business hours |
| P3 | A question or a minor issue | 1 business day |

Customers on other plans receive P1 responses during support phone hours.

## Escalation

If a ticket is not resolved in time, it is escalated first to the support lead, and then to the Head of Customer Success,
Nisha Verma.

## Uptime guarantee (SLA)

The Growth, Pro and Enterprise plans have a 99.9 percent monthly uptime guarantee. The Starter and Basic plans are best effort
and have no uptime guarantee.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9 percent but stays at or above 99.0 percent, the customer receives a service credit of
10 percent of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0 percent, the credit is 25 percent.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance takes place on Sundays from 02:00 to 04:00 IST. Customers are told at least 3 days in advance.
