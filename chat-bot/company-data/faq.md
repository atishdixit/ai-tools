# Frequently asked questions

## Do you offer a free trial?

Yes for RouteWise and StockSense: both include a 14-day free trial and no credit card is needed. Dockly has no free trial,
but you can book a free 30-minute live demo with Sales.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Customers in India can pay by bank transfer (NEFT or RTGS), credit card or UPI. International customers can pay by credit card
or international wire transfer.

## Can I connect Zenith products to my existing systems?

Yes. All products have a REST API. There are ready-made connectors for SAP S/4HANA, Tally and Zoho Inventory. The API
documentation is at docs.zenithcloudworks.example.

## Which languages does the product support?

The product screens are available in English, Hindi and Marathi. Documentation is in English only.

## Is there a mobile app?

There is a driver app for Android and iOS. There is no Windows desktop app; the web application works in any modern browser.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your data as CSV or JSON at any time.

## Can I install the software on my own servers?

No. Zenith Cloudworks does not currently offer on-premise installation. All products are delivered from the cloud.

## How do I get a custom quote for the Enterprise plan?

Email sales@zenithcloudworks.example or call +91 20 5550 0100. Enterprise pricing depends on the number of vehicles, sites and
integrations.
