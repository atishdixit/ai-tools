# Security and compliance

## Certifications and audits

- ISO 27001: Zenith Cloudworks has been ISO 27001 certified since 2022, and the certificate was renewed in 2025.
- SOC 2 Type II: the audit is in progress and is expected to finish in the fourth quarter (Q4) of 2026. The company is not yet
  SOC 2 certified.

## Where customer data is stored

Customer data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Customers in Singapore can choose the
Singapore region (ap-southeast-1) instead.

## Protection of data

- Data is encrypted with AES-256 when stored and with TLS 1.2 or newer when sent over a network.
- Employees can reach production data only through approved and logged sessions. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory.
- Backups run daily and are kept for 35 days. The recovery point objective is 4 hours and the recovery time objective is 8 hours.

## Data retention and deletion

When a customer contract ends, Zenith Cloudworks deletes the customer's data within 60 days. Customers can export their data
in CSV or JSON format at any time before then.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email security@zenithcloudworks.example. The security team replies within 2 business days.

## What is not offered

Zenith Cloudworks does not currently offer on-premise installation. All products run in the cloud.
