# About Zenith Cloudworks

## Overview

Zenith Cloudworks Pvt Ltd is a software company that builds cloud tools for logistics and warehouse teams. The company was
founded in 2016 by Meera Kulkarni and Arjun Rao. Its headquarters is in Pune, India.

Zenith Cloudworks is a fictional company created for this chatbot demo. All names, prices and policies in these files are invented.

## Size and customers

As of January 2026 the company has 240 employees. It serves 410 customer companies in 14 countries.

## Offices

- Pune, India: headquarters, product and customer success teams.
- Bengaluru, India: engineering centre, opened in 2020.
- Singapore: sales office for Southeast Asia, opened in 2024.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make every warehouse run on clear, live data.

Our four values are: customer first, plain speaking, own the outcome, and keep learning.

## Milestones

- 2016: Zenith Cloudworks is founded in Pune.
- 2018: RouteWise, the delivery route optimisation product, is launched.
- 2020: The Bengaluru engineering centre opens.
- 2022: StockSense, the inventory forecasting product, is launched. The company earns ISO 27001 certification.
- 2024: The Singapore sales office opens.
- 2025: Dockly, the dock scheduling product, is launched.
